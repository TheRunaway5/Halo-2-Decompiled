## Narrow integration seam and limits

A native composition can retain the source-created distinct effect/system/location/emitter/particle identities and authored material/atlas records, then feed the existing render-input/sprite/material kernels only when their actual camera/view/context owners are available or explicitly labeled native adapters. The immediate missing original owner is renderer context21 plus its subsequent per-view camera/visibility scheduling. Particle age and normal effect lifetime belong to the separate clock/lifecycle continuation. LAN effect delivery is also separate.

This freeze provides natural negative admission, a source-owned partial renderer constructor and complete static draw ownership. It does not prove a visible muzzle flash, a positive particle-to-sprite callback, a current camera frame, complete title/world rendering, arbitrary shader branches, or1.5 execution. No production, shader, GPU or previous frozen-file changes belong to this extension.
