# Vehicle weapon and seat HUD wiring, October 15

Current wiring and final verification: [the completed October15 report](WEAPON_HUD_WIRING_COMPLETE_20261015.md). The results below retain the earlier delivery evidence.

Continuation of [the player weapon/zoom HUD work](WEAPON_ZOOM_HUD_WIRING_20261014.md).
Original English Xbox TU1.5 is the gameplay authority. Original TU1.1 is a
separate cross-check of the matching ownership decisions.

## Changes

The retained source unit's seat now selects the vehicle weapon HUD. The
selection follows `13B275..13B3F9` / TU1.5 `13C5E5..13C769`: require an actual
parent and seat, use seat flag8 to select the parent's equipped weapon or an
eligible child unit's equipped weapon, and use seat flag20 to retain handheld
HUDs. Source object identities, inventory and child order supply the result.

The original complete TU1.5 `13DA89` and `1055F0` bodies publish the selected
weapon's status. Their 289 instructions are generated by
`tools/generate_weapon_hud_tu15.py`; existing original reserve, target and marker
owners remain required callees. Vehicle ammo, heat, age, charge, overheat and
lock status now reach weapon HUD owner type4 instead of consuming handheld
values. Primary and secondary magazine-working state also reaches the existing
per-weapon projection.

Seat interfaces are a separate original owner. Complete `22E0F0` chooses
campaign interface0 or multiplayer interface1, bounded by the authored count.
The snapshot carries that HUD reference, and the renderer prepares its original
widgets before uploading resources. This matters for the Spectre: its plasma
turret weapon has **NONE** at `weap+2B4`, while its seat references
`ui\hud\spectre`. The first live Spectre test failed before this missing
connection was implemented. No replacement crosshair artwork is authored.

Native LAN protocol82 carries vehicle status, seat HUD reference, handheld
visibility and secondary magazine mode. Decoder checks reject invalid seat and
weapon-state flag bytes. Both peers must use this native protocol version.

Vehicle resource preparation follows each loaded unit's authored weapon list
at `unit+1C0`, including child units and the selected variant, alongside the
seat interface lists. It no longer duplicates the complete handheld weapon
bank. The two-view Spectre capture's resident material count decreased from
40,600 to 23,616 while retaining its original crosshair.

## Verification

Evidence is retained under `analysis/multiplayer/weapon-zoom-hud-20261015/`.
Completed checks:

- Nine focused CTests passed, including all-weapon zoom, target projection,
  bitmap geometry, original weapon status, transport and vehicle ownership.
- 1,296 native/original ownership comparisons; 11 authored vehicle/turret
  crosshairs and 2 seat-owned crosshairs across 78 full/split layout draws.
  The original and retained imported MCC artwork packs both pass.
- Seven live TU1.5 worlds: Ghost, Banshee, Scorpion, Wraith, Warthog chaingun,
  Warthog Gauss and Spectre. Normal original seat entry, actual weapon identity,
  heat/age publication, authored HUD submission, another player's independence
  and complete HUD release on exit pass.
- Six original source worlds and 8 optic/binocular levels pass the retained
  player zoom regression. Actual loopback UDP scope admission, shot/reset,
  stale request and reload lifecycle also pass.
- 128 actual native packet-codec round trips preserve independent hand and
  vehicle state, seat HUD references and offhand magazine mode. Invalid vehicle
  flag bytes are rejected.
- The final Spectre Vulkan test exits successfully after 4 submitted frames on
  an RTX 5080 at 1280x720. `spectre-hud-authored.ppm` is the raw renderer capture;
  its PNG conversion was inspected. The seated view shows the Spectre reticle
  while the other view retains its SMG reticle/ammo HUD.
- The final SDL scope regression passes 450 submitted frames and 119 source
  ticks: actual RMB input, unit/view/HUD selectors, observer FOV transitions,
  shot/reset and manual reload/admission/release.
- The imported crosshair regression retains 31 weapons, 84 authored rows,
  75 selected pages and 20,160 unchanged full/split/dual-hand vertices.
  The source-status generator reproduces its 289-instruction module exactly.

The shared checkout's new constraint-prototype initialization exposed a missing
physics size-query owner during the live tests. Complete original `31F840` and
`325E90` (38 instructions) were added to the existing conveyor generator/module.
The original source-world tests then passed; no size/result or physics state is
injected to get to HUD submission. Earlier incomplete/failing logs are retained.

The initial rendered fixture captured its four frames but timed out while it
continued source stepping after returning from the runtime. The final renderer
fixture ends at the finite render boundary; the separate world-only fixture
verifies entry/exit. No validation layer was enabled, and these captures do not
establish matched Xbox framebuffer pixels or a live enemy lock encounter.
