# Vista menu assets confirmed in MCC

Copyable inventory for the MCC importer conversation. Emblems and button prompts are excluded. Virtual-keyboard keys, controller illustrations and status icons remain included.

**287 Vista menu banks, 648 bank/frame owners, 561 unique texture pages available.** Of these, 645 owners / 558 unique pages have byte-identical DDS files. Three dedicated-server icon owners / pages have identical RGB and alpha differences no larger than 1/255.

Source: `<MCC>/halo2/h2_maps_win64_dx11/*.map` plus `textures.dat`. This list is source availability evidence, not a runtime activation or redistribution-rights determination.

The full page-by-page importer mapping and resource addresses are in `analysis/frontend/vista-menu-vectors/mcc-menu-match-audit-20261013-final/importer-map.tsv`.
