## Remaining native closure

- The real on-foot camera path uses `CD4A0`, including `E18D0` and collision
  query `16C620`. Those services need native kernels and source-data lifetime
  qualification before production admission. An attached-only camera
  candidate exists but remains unqualified and does not cover this input.
- `18A0C0` remains a reachable original controller constructor. The typed
  postconstructor capture does not witness that branch or close its behavior.
- Positive seated, marker and additional controller constraint profiles need
  whole-parent comparisons with qualified native services.
- Exceptional acos and alternate CRT policy `332047/3334F0` remain explicit
  requirements, along with unmasked traps and unsupported FP domains.
- A production native-only service builder, truthful activity transport and
  visible multiplayer validation are required after these dependencies close.
